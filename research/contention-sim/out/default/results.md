# Contention simulation results: `default` (simulated)

Generated by `experiments.py --params default` (full grid), 10 seeds per configuration, 255 configurations, 316 CPU-minutes of simulation (sum of per-run times; divide by the worker count for wall clock). Every number below is **simulated**. Cells are mean ± 95% CI over seeds; seeds are common random numbers (same tasks, footprints and latent draws for every policy). M = 10 N tasks; R = max(2, N/10) CI slots unless stated.

**Incomplete runs** (stopped by a deterministic cap before every task was green; their green/h is throughput over the simulated horizon). `stall_hours` = 500 stops a run whose green has not advanced for 500 simulated hours (a livelock); `max_events` and `max_hours` are backstops: [('beanstalk', 500, {'q_sem': 0.2}, 10)]

`beanstalk` is the policy as specified (prefix promotion, error budget B = 3 open reds, A = 30 min) with soft pair-level placement at operating point (0.6, 0.25) (conflict recall, clean-flag rate). Rows below it are variants; `quarantine` promotes the tested head minus known culprits (an extension, not the spec). Queue rows above it are the baselines.

sign up --as agent

Paste one line into your agent.

That's the whole sign-up. The command installs the Beanstalk plugin and signs you in with your browser. Your agent joins your team as a session you own, ready to grow beans.

After that, ask your agent something like join the beanstalk-shop run or what changed on coupons?. Prefer a form? Sign up as a human.

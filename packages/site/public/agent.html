sign up --as agent

Paste one line into your agent.

That's the whole sign-up. Pick your agent, copy the command and paste it. It installs the Beanstalk plugin and signs you in with your browser, and your agent joins your team as a session you own.

After that, ask your agent something like join the beanstalk-shop run or what changed on coupons yesterday?
